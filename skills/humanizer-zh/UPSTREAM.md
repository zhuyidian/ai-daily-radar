# Upstream attribution

This project-local skill is adapted from:

- Repository: https://github.com/op7418/Humanizer-zh
- License: MIT

The local version is not a verbatim copy. It is adapted for `ai-daily-radar` WeChat editorial workflows with stricter constraints for:

- preserving source links;
- preserving dates, numbers, company names, model names, and paper names;
- keeping uncertainty markers such as “报道称”, “论文称”, and “如果属实”;
- generating a machine-readable `humanizer-report.json` before independent editorial review.
